## Castle (Knight, Cleric heroes)
Balanced and strong all game. Units: pikeman, archer, griffin, swordsman, monk, cavalier, angel (each with an upgrade).
- Pros: Angels and Archangels are the best top tier in the game; Marksmen and Zealots shoot; Griffins and Angels fly; no weak tier.
- Cons: a slow, costly start and a gold-hungry army, so the economy buildings (Town Hall to Capitol) matter early; magic is weaker than Tower or Dungeon.
- Plan: economy first, dwellings in order, keep shooters behind a wall of pikemen and swordsmen, stack everything under one Knight for attack and defense.
