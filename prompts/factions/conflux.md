## Conflux (Planeswalker, Elementalist heroes)
Units: pixie, air elemental, water elemental, fire elemental, earth elemental, psychic elemental, firebird (each with an upgrade).
- Pros: elemental armies resist or ignore many spells; Phoenixes fly and rise again; unusual units mean opponents often misjudge them; strong with the right primary resources.
- Cons: the tiers are narrow and the economy needs a lot of rare resources (mercury, sulfur, crystal, gems), so scouting for them early decides the game.
- Plan: take every rare-resource source early, build the elemental dwellings that your income supports, keep spells for the elementals.
