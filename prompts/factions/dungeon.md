## Dungeon (Overlord, Warlock heroes)
Units: troglodyte, harpy, beholder, medusa, minotaur, manticore, dragon (each with an upgrade).
- Pros: strong magic and the best late dragons (Black Dragons are immune to all magic); Minotaurs hit hard; Troglodytes are cheap; Medusas shoot and petrify.
- Cons: weak, fragile low tiers; needs rare resources (sulfur, crystal, gems, mercury) for the top tiers, and a lot of gold.
- Plan: Capitol early, secure sulfur and crystal, protect the first tiers, Black Dragons win games.
