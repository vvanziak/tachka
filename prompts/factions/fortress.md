## Fortress (Beastmaster, Witch heroes)
Units: gnoll, lizardman, serpent fly, basilisk, gorgon, wyvern, hydra (each with an upgrade).
- Pros: very strong defense: the town and its creatures are built for holding, with a good Wall and Moat; Lizardmen shoot; Hydras hit every adjacent enemy; Wyverns fly and poison; Basilisks petrify.
- Cons: weak magic and a slow, defensive pace; Hydras and Wyverns need rare resources (mercury, sulfur, crystal).
- Plan: hold the town and the early mines, grow into Hydras, then go out and fight.
