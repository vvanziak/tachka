## Inferno (Demoniac, Heretic heroes)
Units: imp, gog, hell hound, demon, pit fiend, efreet, devil (each with an upgrade).
- Pros: cheap units, Gogs and Magogs shoot, Efreeti and Devils are fast and strong; Inferno gets Town Portal-style speed from its fast tiers.
- Cons: the weakest defense and magic resistance; no flyers before the top tiers; Devils and Pit Fiends need rare resources (mercury, sulfur).
- Plan: strike early with the cheap tiers, use the speed to avoid shooters, take sulfur and mercury.
