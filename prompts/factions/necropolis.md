## Necropolis (Death Knight, Necromancer heroes)
Units: skeleton, walking dead, wight, vampire, lich, black knight, bone dragon (each with an upgrade).
- Pros: Necromancy raises Skeletons from the dead after every battle, so the army grows with each fight; undead are immune to mind spells and morale; Liches shoot with a cloud attack; Bone Dragons at the top.
- Cons: no morale bonus and no luck ability; poor against enemies with lots of undead-killing spells and with big shooters.
- Plan: fight early and often, raise skeletons, take mines to build Dread Knights and liches.
