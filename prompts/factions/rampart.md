## Rampart (Ranger, Druid heroes)
Units: centaur, dwarf, wood elf, pegasus, dendroid guard, unicorn, green dragon (each with an upgrade).
- Pros: Wood Elves shoot twice; Dwarves and Unicorns resist magic; Dendroid Guards hold a line; Dragons at the top; good luck and morale tools.
- Cons: the weak first tier and dwarves are slow; Dragons and Unicorns need rare resources, so scouting for crystal and gems early matters.
- Plan: elves behind dendroids, take the rare resource mines early.
