## Stronghold (Barbarian, Battle Mage heroes)
Units: goblin, wolf rider, orc, ogre, roc, cyclops, behemoth (each with an upgrade).
- Pros: the fastest and cheapest start of any faction, so it can snowball with early attacks and take towns; Wolf Riders attack twice; Behemoths reduce enemy defense and cost only gold in the basic form, with no extra resource; Cyclopes shoot and break walls; the Freelancer's Guild sells creatures for resources, which solves a missing-resource problem.
- Cons: no real magic and a weak Mage Guild; low tiers die fast against shooters and big armies; the best units need the Capitol and money.
- Plan: strike early, capture a town (best if it is also Stronghold: double dwellings for the same units), trade creatures for the resources the map does not give you.
