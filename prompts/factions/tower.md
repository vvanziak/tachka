## Tower (Alchemist, Wizard heroes)
Units: gremlin, stone gargoyle, stone golem, mage, naga, genie, giant (each with an upgrade).
- Pros: the best magic of any faction, Mages and Gremlins shoot, Golems and Giants resist spells, Titans shoot hard and are immune to mind spells; strong with a Library and a high Mage Guild.
- Cons: low gold income early, weak melee tiers and a slow start; every unit is expensive, and many need rare resources (mercury, gems, crystal).
- Plan: build the Mage Guild and Library, defend with gargoyles and golems, shoot with gremlins and mages, take a mercury and gem source early.
